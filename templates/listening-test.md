# Listening Test

Listening tests answer questions that measurements do not. They do not replace
measurements, and they are not evidence of electrical performance.

## 1. Question

State one question. Example: "Is clipping character A distinguishable from B at
matched loudness?" Avoid "which sounds better" unless preference is the actual
question.

## 3. Protocol

1. Match levels with a measured method, not by ear.
2. Randomise presentation order.
3. Record every trial, including ties and failures.
4. Do not reveal the mapping until the data is recorded.

## 6. Limits

- A small panel cannot establish a general perceptual rule.
- Preference is not correctness.
- Expectation, level differences and sighted listening are confounds.
- Cite peer-reviewed perceptual evidence for general claims; a single informal
  test is anecdotal.
